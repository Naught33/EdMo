Our Story

From One Neighborhood to Two States

EdMo Elite Property Care was founded on a simple belief: every homeowner deserves contractor-grade results without the contractor runaround. What started as a small roofing and painting operation in the greater Marietta area has grown into a full-service residential renovation company trusted across Tennessee and Georgia.

We've built our reputation one project at a time — showing up on schedule, communicating clearly, and never cutting corners on material quality or workmanship. Our team brings deep trade expertise across roofing, stucco, flooring, bathroom remodeling, decking, and more.

"Elite" isn't just in our name. It's the standard we hold ourselves to on every job, whether it's a 200-square-foot bathroom refresh or a full exterior envelope replacement.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

01

Craftsmanship First

We treat every home as if it were our own. Our crews are trained tradespeople who take pride in clean lines, proper technique, and finishes that last for decades — not just until the check clears.

02

Honest Communication

No surprise invoices, no vague timelines. From the initial quote to final walkthrough, we keep you informed at every stage. If something changes on the job, you hear it from us first.

03

Premium Materials Only

We source high-grade roofing systems, moisture-resistant stucco, hardwood flooring, and composite decking materials — because the longevity of your home depends on what goes into it.

04

Respect for Your Home

We arrive on time, protect your property during every phase of work, and leave each job site cleaner than we found it. Your home is your most valuable asset — we treat it accordingly.

05

Safety & Compliance

Every project is executed to code. We carry full liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage so that you're protected — and so are the people working on your property.

06

Community Roots

We're not a national chain dispatching strangers to your door. We live and work in Tennessee and Georgia, and our reputation in these communities means everything to us.
