More on Stucco

Most stucco damage starts behind the wall. Our remediation process doesn't just patch the surface — we expose the structure, replace any rotted wood, and install premium waterproofing to protect your home for decades to come.

Know the Signs

Don't Wait for a Leak: 5 Signs Your Stucco Needs Professional Repair

  • Staining or "Stucco Tears": Dark streaks or brownish tears from window corners or roof meets — water is trapped behind the surface.
  • Hairline Cracks or Bulges: Any crack wider than a credit card, or a bubbling appearance, indicates stucco pulling away from the house.
  • Cracking at the Foundation (Weep Screeds): Cracks or moisture at the base of your walls may mean a blocked drainage system and potential wood rot.
  • Soft Spots: If the exterior wall feels spongy when pressed, the underlying wood sheathing is likely already moisture-damaged.
  • Efflorescence (White Crusty Powder): A salty white buildup is a sign that water is migrating through the stucco and evaporating, leaving minerals behind.
